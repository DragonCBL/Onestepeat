餐厅介绍

About Us

RG August 坐落于爱丁堡Calder Park，是一家地道英国中餐外卖店。我们坚持选用新鲜食材，传承粤式、川式、泰式等多元中餐烹饪技艺。从浓汤靓汤、酸甜菜品、咖喱、芙蓉蛋，到宫保、四川风味、烧味与各类炒饭炒面，我们为本地食客带来丰富的中式美味。支持外卖配送与到店自取，欢迎电话或线上下单。

RG August is a local authentic Chinese takeaway located in Calder Park, Edinburgh. We use fresh ingredients and blend Cantonese, Szechuan and Thai‑inspired cooking styles. Our extensive menu includes soups, sweet‑sour dishes, curries, egg foo yung, kung po, szechuan roasts, fried rice and chow mein. We offer delivery and collection service. You are welcome to order online or by phone.
