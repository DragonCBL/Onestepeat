About Us

At RG August, every detail tells a story.

The name RG comes from the initials of our two children, Rita and Gilbert. They are the inspiration behind everything we do and remind us why this journey began.

August holds a special place in our hearts. Both of our children were born in August, and as the eighth month of the year, August also connects naturally with the number 8. In Chinese culture, the number 8 is a symbol of prosperity, good fortune and new beginnings. For us, it represents not only luck, but also family, hope and the dream of building something meaningful together.

RG August is more than a Chinese takeaway. It is a family business built on love, tradition and a passion for sharing authentic Chinese flavours with our local community.

Every meal we prepare is made with the same care and pride as if we were cooking for our own family. We hope that whenever you visit RG August, you will enjoy not only delicious food, but also the warmth, happiness and good fortune that inspired our name.

Welcome to the RG August family.
